VVC in HEIF

For storage of high resolution, high definition images, the HEIF standard allows the use of Versatile Video Coding (VVC or H.266) encoded media. The VVC standard aims to provide further improvements in compression, outperforming other codecs especially with highest definition content. Below examples demonstrate the use of VVC in HEIF, to store 8K and 4K images, compressing them with a high ratio, retaining the quality of the original lossless image to a very high degree. Low overhead Image File Format (SlimHEIF)

SlimHEIF is the latest advancement in file size compression in the HEIF domain. The low overhead amendment to the HEIF standard [ISO/IEC 23008-12] defines a new file level structure that enables removal of redundant metadata from the file. This results in files storing small and simple images, such as icons, thumbnails or emojis, to be significantly smaller in size than their original HEIF counterparts. Experimental results on the topic show that a reduction of 30% to 80% in file size is achievable with SlimHEIF, presenting it to be a de facto standard for its applications.
